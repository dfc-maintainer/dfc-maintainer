## Dunedin Fencing Club Maintainer

We are the website maintainer for Dunedin Fencing Club, an inclusive community fencing club in Edinburgh, Scotland. We were set up by local fencers during the first Covid lockdown in 2020 to continue sharing their love of meeting up each week to hit each other with swords while other activities were being closed down.
We believe fencing is there to support your health and wellbeing, and our classes and club culture are set up to achieve those goals.
